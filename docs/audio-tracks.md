# Audio tracks and mixing

Import lists every audio stream, including language, title, codec, channel layout and sample rate. Listen to a 15-second sample from a chosen time before assigning roles. Select one dialogue source: **Mixed** for dialogue/music/effects together, or **Dialogue** for a clean dialogue stem. Alternative language mixes and commentary should be **Ignore**. Assign genuinely separate non-dialogue streams to **Background**, **Music** or **Effects**. Stream metadata alone cannot reliably determine these roles.

For an existing project, use **Engines → Configure audio tracks → Prepare audio**. Preparation extracts the selected streams and optionally separates the mixed source. It preserves segment boundaries, translations, speaker assignments and generated speech. Choosing another dialogue source does not automatically replace the transcript; use Reanalyze explicitly when that is intended. Changed track settings require preparation before playback, synthesis or export.

BS-RoFormer produces vocals and an instrumental residual for a mixed source. ASR and per-segment source references use the isolated vocals when available. Explicit background streams take precedence over the estimated residual, avoiding duplicate music. Preparation caches completed separation results and checks the original source fingerprint. It does not run TTS again.

**Play** and export share the same mix builder. The timeline provides fixed controls beside Original dialogue, Music/ambience/effects and Dubbed dialogue. Original dialogue is muted by default; generated dialogue and background are enabled. Each group has gain, mute and **Listen solo** controls. Solo is only an audition and does not change exported settings. Speaker icons toggle track sound: waves mean enabled, a crossed speaker means muted. Changing a gain or speaker while playing rebuilds the preview and resumes at the same position after a short preparation; while paused, it stays paused until Play. Pause resumes the same mix; Stop resets the position but preserves mix settings. To hear the original, unmute Original dialogue and mute Dubbed dialogue. To hear the dub, reverse those two mutes. Enabling both intentionally overlays both voices. New projects use background gain 1; existing projects retain their saved gain. Missing background is never replaced with original mixed audio. When isolated vocals are unavailable and the user explicitly unmutes the original track, it is labeled Original audio (full mix); this plays the selected original source and omits additional background to avoid duplication. Separation is required to control its dialogue and music independently.

The preview uses source video and the mixed audio in one temporary MKV, keeping video, sound and seeking on the same player clock. The source player selects the configured audio track. The background waveform combines all selected background streams. Preview permits missing generated segments, while export validates generated audio and duration fit.

Direct background tracks retain their native channel count during extraction, including surround, and the final mix accommodates the selected source/background channel counts up to eight channels. Model separation uses a stereo downmix. It cannot reconstruct the original surround placement. BS-RoFormer estimates stems: dialogue remnants, lost effects or other artifacts remain possible. Listen to the prepared background and prefer an authentic music/effects stem when available. This workflow does not infer dialogue from a 5.1 center channel, since that channel may also contain music and effects.

Validation: automated multitrack fixtures check metadata, explicit selection, preserved edits, ignored alternate mixes, six-channel backgrounds, separation caching, no duplicate estimated background, mute/solo behavior, preview/export consistency and project persistence. A real eight-second Blade Runner excerpt was separated by the installed BS-RoFormer CUDA runtime into vocals and instrumental files. That execution check does not establish perceptual separation quality.
